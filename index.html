How CheckAm uses your data
  • You can use CheckAm without signing up. Your list of checks stays on your phone.
  • When you scan a pack, we save the pack's product number, batch, expiry, serial number, the town you set, the result, and a random number for your phone. We don't save your name, phone number or location from GPS.
  • This lets CheckAm warn people when the same code shows up in different places, and show alerts about suspicious packs.
  • If you sign up for alerts, we keep your name (if given), your WhatsApp number or email, and your state, only to send you alerts. We never sell or share them.
  • Tap "Stop updates" at any time and we stop sending alerts.
  • Data is stored with Supabase, a database service, on servers in the United States.
